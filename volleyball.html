Game Format

The Runs

  • 2 courts, capped at 24 players
  • 4 teams — pre-made rosters by skill level & position
  • King's Court — Winners & Losers courts
  • 15-minute games, played to 25 or the time limit

All Levels Welcome

Teams are balanced by skill and position, so beginners through advanced players all get competitive, fun runs. The sign-up form has a quick skills questionnaire to help us keep teams even.

E-Transfer

Payment is by e-transfer to secure your spot. The address and note format are included in the sign-up form.

How spots work: weekly polls are posted in the designated WhatsApp group, and payment secures your spot. Spots are limited and first come, first served — if there's a waitlist, unpaid spots may be given away at the weekly deadline.
🚫 Refund policy: all payments are final, non-refundable and non-transferable. If you can't attend a session, please notify the organizers in advance.
