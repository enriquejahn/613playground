About Us

613 Playground Junior is the youth side of 613 Playground — Ottawa's growing sports community. What started as friends getting together to hoop has grown into a league, drop-in runs, and now a Sunday-morning program for our youngest players. We keep it simple: fun first, fundamentals always — in a positive environment where every kid gets touches, reps and encouragement.

🏀

Fun First

A positive, high-energy environment where every kid gets touches, reps and encouragement — and leaves wanting to come back.

🎯

The Fundamentals

Dribbling, passing, shooting and footwork through games and drills that build real skills and confidence.

👟

Every Kid Plays

Led by experienced basketball players from our community — including a coach with an education background. Parents are welcome to stay courtside.
